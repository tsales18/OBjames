In English _'s_ has different uses.

**1.** is  
Here _'s = is._  
_Where_**_'s_** _he from?_ (= _Where_ **_is_** _he from._)  
_He_**_'s_** _Spanish._ (= _He_ **_is_** _Spanish_.)

**2.** has  
Here _'s = has_.  
_Sue_**_'s_** _got two brothers._ (= _Sue_ **_has_** _got two brothers_.)  
_Alan_**_'s_** _got a big car_. (= _Alan_ **_has_** _got a big car._)

**3.** possession  
Here _'s_ means possession.  
_Ann_**_'s_** _husband comes from Spain._ (= the husband of Ann)  
_My brother'_**_s_** _job is very difficult_. (= the job that my brother does)

Note - we don't use _'s_ for plurals or present simple verbs.

_Ann likes all sports._ (NOT _Ann like_**_'s_** _all sport's._)
