
When we say that we like something, we use a verb with the ending -_ing._

_I like watch_**_ing_** _TV.  
Do you like read_**_ing_**_?_

We do the same with other verbs to describe what we like or don't like doing.

_She loves play_**_ing_** _golf.  
I hate clean_**_ing_** _my home._

1. Maya, what do you do in your free time?  
     
2. What do you like doing in summer?  
     
3. Well, I love riding my bike.  
     
4. I often go to the cinema in winter.  
     
5. And I like running in winter too. I don’t get cold when I'm running.  
     
6. Do you like reading books in winter?  
     
7. Do you swim in the sea?  
     
8. In summer I love swimming in the sea.  
     
9. I don’t like shopping.  
     
10. My friends and I play football every week in winter.
