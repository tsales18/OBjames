There are different ways to make questions:

**1.** with the verb _be_:

_Ming is Chinese.  
Is Ming Chinese?_

_Sue and Astrid are teachers.  
Are Sue and Astrid teachers?_  
 

**2.** with other verbs we use an extra verb, an auxiliary verb. The most common auxiliary verb is do /does:

_Ming and his wife watch TV in the evening.  
Do Ming and his wife watch TV in the evening?_

_Ming works in Shanghai.  
Does Ming work in Shanghai?_  
 

**3.** with question words and the auxiliary do/does:

_Where do Sue and Astrid work?  
What do you do in the evening?  
How does Astrid get to work?  
What time do you start work?  
How long does it take to get to work?_
