When we say how well we can do something we use the word at.

_Pete's good_ **_at_** _skateboarding._  
_Elena's good_ **_at_** _playing basketball._

In addition to good we use other words:

_Namisha's_ **_brilliant at_** _tennis. She played at Wimbledon last year._  
_Tom's_ **_OK at_** _swimming._  
_​David's_ **_terrible at_** _rugby._
